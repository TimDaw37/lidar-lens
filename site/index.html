      How to use the LiDAR Lens

      The circle is a lens showing the 1 m LiDAR ground model over the aerial photo. Drag its rim or top grip to move it; drag the white dot to resize. Use Go to for a grid ref, postcode or place, or Use my location.

      What is it? shows the shape of the ground (measured from the LiDAR) and nearby records for the spot you click. Elevation profile: click two points.
