Stonehenge ground change, 2001–2021

This map compares two Environment Agency LiDAR surveys of the Stonehenge landscape, from 2001 and 2021. The coloured layer is the 2021 ground height minus the 2001 height. The surveys were lined up on roads and other hard surfaces, and the result was smoothed over 5 m.

10 cm lowerno change10 cm higher

Blue areas are lower now and red areas are higher. The fine stripes left by ploughing in 2001 have gone where fields were put back to grass. No new earthworks show up. Changes of a few centimetres sit close to the limits of the data, so treat small patches with caution.
